## Decisions and constraints

- The daemon is the sole owner of live runtime state; MCP, CLI, dashboard, and Tauri are clients.
- `nomoreide-daemon-client` must remain directly and transitively core-free.
- Preserve argument-vector execution, protected-process and expected-command checks, loopback-only access, private credentials, and MCP stdout purity.
- Do not expose arbitrary command execution or broad process mutation yet.
- TypeScript is the behavioral reference until black-box parity passes; similarly named Rust/Tauri code is not proof of parity.
- Secure client behavior in `32bba80` includes `no_proxy`, bounded requests, state/health owner matching, per-request revalidation, and owner pinning. Do not weaken it.
